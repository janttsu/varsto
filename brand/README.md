# Varsto brand

One mark, drawn from one construction, at every size. Everything in this
directory, the website logo and favicon, and the Android launcher icons are
generated by `render.py`; edit the geometry there, not the output files.

## The mark: "the dial"

A round vault door seen head-on: one ring broken into two arcs (the sync loop,
two copies passing each other) with a keyhole in the middle (the lock, your
key). White on a blue tile. It reads as *vault* and *sync* at once, carries no
letters, and stays legible from a 16 px favicon to a 1024 px store icon.

### Construction (master grid 256 x 256)

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Tile | 256 x 256, corner radius 57 (22.4 %, the iOS/macOS squircle ratio) |
| Ring | centre-line radius 70, stroke 20, round caps |
| Gaps | centred at 45 degrees upper right and 225 degrees lower left, 24 units cap to cap |
| Keyhole head | circle radius 22, centre 16 above the tile centre |
| Keyhole stem | half-width 10 at the head, 14 at the foot, foot 40 below the tile centre, corner radius 4 |
| Glyph colour | white; the glyph is one filled path (no strokes), so it survives every converter |

The keyhole's optical centre, not its bounding box, sits on the tile centre:
the heavier head is raised so the shape does not look as if it is sinking.

### Size tiers

The same construction with weights tuned per pixel size, so small sizes are
crisp instead of smeared. `render.py` picks the tier from the output size.

| Tier | Used at | Ring stroke | Gap | Head radius | Stem |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| large | 128 px and up, every vector | 20 | 24 | 22 | 10 / 14 |
| medium | 32 to 64 px | 24 | 32 | 24 | 12 / 16 |
| small | 16 to 24 px (favicon, tray) | 30 | 52 | 30 | 16 straight (2 px at 16 px) |
| menubar | macOS 22 pt template, glyph only | 20 | 34 | 23 | 11 / 15 |

The small tier is aligned to the 16 px pixel grid: the stem is exactly 2 px
wide and the head a 4 px dot.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal blue | `#2b57d6` | the tile (flat), site accent |
| Vault blue | `#1b3a9a` | deep end of the palette; the app icon gradient runs 60 % of the way to it (`#2146b2`) |
| White | `#ffffff` | the glyph on the tile; the glyph alone on dark backgrounds |
| Ink | `#16181d` | wordmark on light backgrounds; the mono glyph |
| Paper | `#eceef2` | wordmark on dark backgrounds |

The gradient (top `#2b57d6` to bottom `#2146b2`, vertical) is used only on
the app icon at 128 px and above (`appicon.svg`, the large PNGs, the ICNS and
the Android legacy mipmaps). Everything that can be shown small is flat.

## Wordmark and lockup

`wordmark.svg` / `wordmark-dark.svg`: the tile mark (M = 96) followed by
"Varsto" in Inter SemiBold set as outlines (self-contained, no font needed).

- Cap height = 0.6 M, vertically centred on the mark.
- Gap between mark and text = 0.4 M.
- Tracking: -3 % of the em, letters kerned with HarfBuzz.
- The viewBox is tight to the artwork; keep clear space of at least 0.5 M
  around the lockup.

The website header uses `logo.svg` at 28 px with its own text; that matches
the lockup proportions closely (the mark, then the name at roughly 0.6 M cap
height).

## Files

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `logo.svg` | the mark on a flat tile, 256 viewBox; the master (website header, Linux hicolor icon) |
| `appicon.svg` | the same with the subtle app icon gradient, for renders at 128 px and above |
| `logo-small.svg` | small tier on a 16 viewBox; the favicon |
| `logo-mono.svg` | glyph only, ink, for one-colour print and light backgrounds without a tile |
| `logo-dark.svg` | glyph only, white, for dark backgrounds without a tile |
| `menubar-template.svg` | glyph only, black, 22 x 22, 18 px outer diameter: the macOS menu bar template image (set `isTemplate`) |
| `wordmark.svg`, `wordmark-dark.svg` | lockups for light and dark backgrounds |
| `png/logo-{16,32,48,64,128,256,512,1024}.png` | full-bleed tile renders |
| `varsto.ico` | 16/32/48/256 for Windows |
| `Varsto.icns` | macOS app icon: the tile at 80.5 % of the canvas with a soft shadow, Apple's layout, all eleven slots |
| `render.py` | generates all of the above, the website copies and the Android icons |

Android: `apps/android/.../mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml` is an adaptive
icon with a flat Signal-blue background, the glyph as foreground at 52 dp
outer diameter (inside the 66 dp safe zone) and a monochrome layer for themed
icons; `mipmap-*/ic_launcher.png` (48 to 192 px) is the legacy fallback.

## Do and don't

- Do use `logo.svg` on both light and dark backgrounds; the tile carries its
  own contrast.
- Do use the glyph-only files when a tile would look heavy (mono print, a
  dark splash screen, the menu bar).
- Do keep clear space of 0.5 M around the mark and the lockup.
- Don't put the glyph on a tile of another colour, rotate it, outline it, or
  add text inside the tile.
- Don't recolour the keyhole separately from the ring; the mark is one colour
  on one tile.
- Don't stretch the lockup or change the mark-to-text ratio; scale the whole
  file.
- Don't use the gradient below 128 px.

## Regenerate

```sh
python3 brand/render.py           # writes every file listed above
python3 brand/render.py --check   # parses every SVG, renders it, checks PNG sizes and the ICNS
python3 brand/render.py --sheet /tmp/sheet.png   # contact sheet for a visual review
```

Needs `rsvg-convert` (librsvg), `magick` (ImageMagick 7), Pillow, fontTools
and, for a kerned wordmark, `uharfbuzz`. The wordmark is set from
`website/tools/render/fonts/Inter-SemiBold.ttf`.
