Varsto and Tresorit

Tresorit is end-to-end encrypted cloud storage and file sharing from Switzerland, aimed mainly at businesses and regulated sectors, with personal plans as well. Its clean, careful interface is one of the inspirations for Varsto's own.

How Tresorit works

You create an account and files are stored in Tresorit's data centres, with data-residency choices for business customers. Files and relevant metadata are encrypted end to end; keys are shared with public-key cryptography. It offers encrypted share links, version history (the number of versions depends on the plan), remote device wipe and apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS and the web.

Which one fits

Tresorit

Choose Tresorit when you need a compliant, supported business service with administration, data residency and polished sharing, and a closed-source provider is acceptable.

Varsto

Choose Varsto when you want comparable end-to-end encryption without a provider in the middle: your own buckets and disks, peer-to-peer transfer between your devices, a record of every copy, and source you can read. Varsto's organisation features (administrators, approvals, removing a person) are new and still alpha.

Varsto is alpha software: do not trust it with data you have no other copy of, and its cryptography has not been independently audited. The other products here are mature. Facts about them were checked from their own documentation in October 2026; prices and plans change often, so see their own pages for current figures.
