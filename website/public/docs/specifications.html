Status of the documents that will define formats and protocols. Sections 32 to 60 of alpha-0-format.md are on main new in 0.0.1-alpha.14. Most of what exists is described in alpha-0-format.md, section by section; anything marked not started has no implementation. They are intended to be open so that implementations remain interoperable and data stays recoverable.
