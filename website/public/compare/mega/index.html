Varsto and MEGA

MEGA is end-to-end encrypted cloud storage for consumers and businesses, with generous storage plans, desktop sync, mobile apps and related services such as chat and a VPN.

How MEGA works

You create an account and your files are stored in MEGA's data centres; devices sync through MEGA's servers. Files are encrypted on the client with per-file keys under a key derived from your password. In 2022 researchers at ETH Zurich published attacks on MEGA's cryptography; MEGA patched them, and the authors noted that a complete fix would need a redesign. There are desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux, mobile apps and a command-line client.

Which one fits

MEGA

Choose MEGA when you want a lot of encrypted cloud space from one provider, with polished apps everywhere, and you are comfortable with the provider holding the only off-site copy.

Varsto

Choose Varsto when you want to pick the storage yourself (or several providers at once), avoid lock-in, keep copies on your own disks as well, and know at every moment where each copy is.

Varsto is alpha software: do not trust it with data you have no other copy of, and its cryptography has not been independently audited. The other products here are mature. Facts about them were checked from their own documentation in October 2026; prices and plans change often, so see their own pages for current figures.
